Questions merchants and sales ask

What changed for a migrated senangPay merchant? Native

Their recurring billing runs on FlexiBill: plans, subscriptions, invoices, receipts and card auto-debit sit in one DOKU dashboard.

Do customers have to enter their card again? Validation required

Saved cards carried over as tokens, so charging continues without customers re-entering details. If a card is declined or expires, the customer gets an email asking them to update it. Confirm each merchant's status with customer success before you promise anything.

Which payment methods can customers in Malaysia use? Country constrained

Card auto-debit for recurring bills. Over-the-counter and link payment for Malaysia are Q4 2026 roadmap items. PayChat is not available in Malaysia.

How do customers receive their bills? Country constrained

By email. Malaysia is email-first for invoices, receipts and reminders.

Is there a self-service portal for customers? Roadmap

Billing Web comes first, then the Billing Portal for Malaysia, planned for January 2027. Until then, customers act through the links in their emails.

How is tax shown on invoices? Configurable

Invoices carry SST, set in the merchant's tax settings. MyInvois e-invoicing is a 2027 proposal.

What happens when a payment fails? Roadmap

Smart Retry and reminders for Malaysia are Q4 2026 items. Quote only the approved Malaysia recovery range from the Sales Hub, never the Indonesian figure.

Can a merchant change plans, prices or billing dates? Configurable

Plans, prices, coupons and subscription changes are managed from the dashboard. Calendar billing and proration for Malaysia are Q4 2026 items.

Who handles a billing issue a merchant reports? Process

Customer success owns the merchant and raises product issues to the FlexiBill product team. Do not promise fixes or dates yourself.

Can new Malaysian merchants join FlexiBill directly? Roadmap

Yes. New merchants are sold on FlexiBill with the Malaysia playbooks. senangPay merchants move to native onboarding from Q1 2027.

What must I never promise? Guardrail

PayChat, direct debit, the Billing Portal date, MyInvois, or any recovery number outside the approved Malaysia range.

Which files can I send to a merchant? Guardrail

Only files marked Merchant-shareable: the For Merchants deck, the Merchant Playbook and the merchant pricing guides. Never forward files marked Internal.
