07 — FAQ

Pertanyaan Merchant: Implementasi & OperasionalMerchant FAQ: Implementation & Operations

Pertanyaan yang paling sering diajukan merchant sebelum go-live. Klik pertanyaan untuk melihat jawaban.Most frequently asked questions by merchants before go-live. Click to see the answer.

1) Apa itu Pricing Table di FlexiBill?What is a Pricing Table in FlexiBill?
Pricing Table adalah halaman pemilihan plan yang di-host dan dibuat dari satu FlexiBill Collection. Merchant bisa mempresentasikan plan, harga, fitur, promosi, pajak, dan tombol checkout dalam satu halaman yang bisa di-share atau di-embed — tanpa membangun halaman pricing kustom dari nol.A Pricing Table is a hosted plan-selection page generated from one FlexiBill Collection. It lets a merchant present plans, prices, features, promotions, taxes, and checkout CTA in one shareable or embeddable page — without building a custom pricing page from scratch.
2) Apakah butuh developer untuk launch?Do I need a developer to launch?
Tidak harus. Merchant bisa membuat Pricing Table dari dashboard dengan memilih Collection, plan, mengatur tampilan, dan publish. Engineering hanya diperlukan untuk integrasi lebih dalam, analytics kustom, atau embedded experience di luar halaman standard.Not necessarily. A merchant can create a Pricing Table from the dashboard — select a Collection, choose plans, configure the appearance, and publish. Engineering is only needed for deeper website integration, custom analytics, or embedded experiences beyond the standard page.
3) Bisakah saya embed Pricing Table di website saya?Can I embed the Pricing Table on my website?
