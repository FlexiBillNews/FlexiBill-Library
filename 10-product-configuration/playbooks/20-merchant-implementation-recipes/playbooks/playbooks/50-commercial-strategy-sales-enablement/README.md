# Commercial Strategy, Sales Enablement & Merchant Growth

## Purpose

This playbook helps DOKU teams translate FlexiBill's billing and subscription capabilities into clear merchant value propositions, qualified opportunities, practical solution designs, and sustainable commercial growth.

It is designed for:
- Sales and Business Development
- Product and Product Marketing
- Partnership and Strategic Alliances
- Merchant Success and Account Management
- Solution Engineering and Presales
- Country and Regional Management

## Commercial discipline

All commercial recommendations must distinguish between:

- **Available:** Confirmed capability that can be represented as currently available.
- **Conditional:** Capability dependent on configuration, integration, merchant setup, country, or commercial approval.
- **Validation required:** Capability or operating detail that must be confirmed with the relevant DOKU team.
- **Gap:** A known limitation or missing capability.
- **Future / Strategic:** A target direction or concept, not a representation of current availability.

Do not promise a capability, integration, service level, country coverage, or commercial term without the appropriate internal validation.

## Core commercial principle

FlexiBill should be positioned around the merchant's business outcome — not simply as a recurring payment feature.

The commercial conversation should connect:

1. The merchant's billing model.
2. The customer's payment experience.
3. The merchant's collection and operational process.
4. The payment methods and integration model.
5. The measurable commercial opportunity.
6. The implementation effort and operational ownership.

## Country considerations

Indonesia and Malaysia must be assessed separately. Similarity in business use cases does not establish identical product availability, payment methods, regulatory requirements, integrations, or operating procedures.

Country-specific statements must be validated with the relevant DOKU product, technology, operations, risk, and commercial stakeholders.
