🇲🇾 FlexiBill for Malaysia · Step-by-step

Set up recurring billing that runs on its own.

A plain-language guide for merchants. Create a Recurring Subscription (bills forever until you stop it) or a Recurring Installment (bills a fixed number of times, then stops) — and share one link your customers can subscribe through.

Path: Subscription & Billing → Collection → Plan → Pricing Table Currency: RM (MYR) 8 steps + 1 setting changes everything
The only real difference between the two

A subscription keeps generating a new bill every cycle — no end date — until you cancel it.

i

What you'll build

FlexiBill handles the whole customer lifecycle for you — recurring billing, renewals and memberships — and sends each invoice automatically. You set the rules once; FlexiBill raises the bills on schedule.

This guide covers the self-serve subscription setup: you create a plan, FlexiBill gives you a Pricing Table (a ready-made page), and you share its link. Customers pick a plan, enter their details, and turn on auto-debit — every future bill then collects on its own.

You will do everything from one menu in the DOKU dashboard: Subscription & Billing under FlexiBill. If you don't see it yet, activate FlexiBill first (a registered Business account that has completed verification — personal accounts can't activate it).

i

How FlexiBill fits together

Four things, in order. Each one lives inside the one before it — like folders inside folders.

∞  NEVER ENDS

A · Recurring Subscription

Bills the same amount every cycle and renews forever — until you (or the customer) cancel. Perfect for memberships and ongoing services.

Example: FitHaus Studio monthly membership — RM 150 every month, no end date.
▣  STOPS AFTER N

B · Recurring Installment

Bills on the same cycle but stops automatically after a set number of bills. Perfect for splitting one price into equal payments.

Example: FitHaus Annual Pass — RM 125 × 12 months, then it stops on its own.

Before the plan: create a Collection

A Collection is the box your plans live in. Make this first — your plan needs somewhere to go, and the Collection sets two rules that decide how long customers can act on each bill.

0
Subscription & Billing → Collection → Create

Fill in four fields

Open Subscription & Billing, go to Collection, and click Create Collection. Four things:

1. Name of Collection — what you'll recognise it by later, e.g. FitHaus Memberships 2025.

2. Collection Description — a short note on what's inside.

3. Collection Invoice Term — how long the invoice link stays open for the customer to view and pay.

4. Grace Period — how long the subscription keeps working after a bill is due but not yet paid, before it's treated as overdue.

=

Invoice Term + Grace Period = how long the checkout link lives. A customer's pay-now link expires based on these two settings. Set them once here and every bill in this Collection inherits them.

✓

A common, safe starting point for monthly plans: Invoice Term 7 days, Grace Period 3 days. Short enough to keep cash flow tight, long enough that customers aren't locked out over a weekend.

Now the plan itself. Inside your Collection, click Create Subscription Plan and walk through these eight steps. We'll build FitHaus Studio — Monthly Membership as we go.

Name, description & image

✓

Step 2 · Pricing model

Pick how the price is calculated

FlexiBill offers five pricing models. Most memberships are simply Flat. The others matter when price depends on quantity (seats, licences, members).

Tap each one in the explorer below to see what it means and a worked RM example. Then pick the one that matches how you charge.

Not sure? Choose Flat. One fixed price per cycle, regardless of quantity — the right answer for the vast majority of subscriptions.

Step 3 · Amount

Create the price

Enter the actual amount and set the currency to MYR (RM). For a Flat membership that's a single number, e.g. RM 150.00.

If you chose a quantity-based model in Step 2, you'll enter the brackets here instead (e.g. 1–5 seats at one rate, 6+ at another).

!

Check the currency before saving. Malaysian plans must be MYR. Amounts are in Ringgit — RM 150, not sen.

Step 4 · Cadence

Billing cycle & interval

Set Billing Cycle to Recurring — this is what tells FlexiBill to keep raising bills instead of charging once.

Then choose the Billing Interval — how often a bill is raised: Daily, Weekly, or Custom (e.g. every 30 days, every 3 months, yearly). For FitHaus we'll bill monthly.

i

The interval is the heartbeat of the plan. A Monthly interval means a fresh invoice — with a new invoice number — is generated every month on the billing date.

Step 5 · Start

Select the date of billing

Choose when the first bill goes out and which day each cycle lands on. You can bill on a fixed interval from the start date, or on a specific calendar date (e.g. the 1st of every month, regardless of when the customer joined).

Pick the start date — for us, 2025-07-01.

!

Don't set a start date in the past. The billing date must be on or after today. Use the format YYYY-MM-DD.

Step 6 · Duration — the deciding setting

Set duration to Unlimited

This single choice is what makes the plan a subscription. Set the duration to Unlimited (sometimes shown as Never ends).

FlexiBill will keep generating a new bill every cycle, with no stop date, until you cancel the subscription manually.

∞

Remember this screen. It's the only place Recurring Subscription and Recurring Installment differ. Part B comes back to exactly this field.

Step 7 · Pricing Table → Collection → Create Pricing Table

Build the Pricing Table

Your plan is saved — now wrap it in a page customers can buy from. In Subscription & Billing → Collection, click Create Pricing Table, then configure it:

Name it (unique). Pick the Collection, then select the plans to show — up to 10 plans per table.

Group by frequency (Daily / Monthly / Yearly) and optionally highlight one featured plan (limit: one per table).

Customise appearance — title (max 50 chars), subtitle (max 256), button label (max 50), plus button & font colours.

Optionally add coupons (up to 10) and apply your pre-configured tax. Watch the live preview on the right update as you go.

✓

Match the button colour to your brand and keep the subtitle to one line on mobile. The live preview is the customer's real view — if it looks right there, it's right.

Step 8 · Integration → URL

Get the share URL

Save the table, then open it from the Pricing Table list (the ⋮ menu → See Details). In the Integration tab you'll find your direct URL and an embed code.

Copy the URL and share it — WhatsApp, email, social bio, or a button on your website. That link is your storefront for this product. You can re-copy it any time from the list, and monitor each table's Status there.

✓

The four detail tabs: Integration (URL + embed), Plan (what's included), Appearance (titles & colours), Additional Details (coupons & tax). Everything is editable later.

Create a Recurring Installment

Good news: you already know how. A Recurring Installment is built with the exact same eight steps — Collection, name, pricing, price, recurring cycle, billing date, Pricing Table, URL. Only Step 6 (Duration) changes.

The one change: at Step 6, instead of Unlimited, choose "Ends after a specific number of bills" and enter the count.

That's it. FlexiBill raises the bill on every cycle just like a subscription, but once it hits the number you set, it stops automatically — no need to remember to cancel. Use it to split one total into equal payments.

What your customer sees

After you share the URL, this is the journey on their side — no app, no account setup with you, just the link.

1

Open & choose

They open your Pricing Table link, pick a plan, and adjust quantity — the total updates instantly. They can apply a coupon if you added one.

2

Verify email

They enter their email and confirm with a one-time password (OTP) sent to their inbox, then fill in personal & billing details.

3

Turn on auto-debit

They toggle Enroll in Auto-Debit and add a card (or a supported direct-debit mandate) so future cycles collect automatically.

4

Pay & confirm

They pay via their chosen channel and get a success screen plus a receipt by email. Every later bill repeats this automatically.

i

Payment channels in Malaysia. Available options can include FPX online banking, cards, e-wallets (Touch ’n Go eWallet, GrabPay, Boost), DuitNow QR, and Virtual Account. For auto-debit, the customer needs a card or a supported direct-debit mandate — a one-time Virtual Account transfer can't be auto-deducted on its own, so it suits manual or single payments rather than hands-off recurring collection.

✓

You watch it all from Subscription & Billing: bill status flips to paid as money lands, each cycle mints a new invoice number, and the dashboard shows what you've collected versus what's still outstanding.
