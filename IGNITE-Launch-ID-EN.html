IGNITE ArenaFlexiBill sales hub

Good news: IGNITE Arena is live.

Everything you need to sell FlexiBill now lives in one place: short lessons, the presentation and a live demo, plays for 10 industries and every deck from the FlexiBill Drive. It’s set up for Indonesia, in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and it’s open to every DOKU team from today.

Enter IGNITE Arena
Read the quick-start guide

What’s inside

Learn
16 short lessons in four paths, each with a quick check. Five levels, from Explain to Lead.
Moments
Short demos and videos that turn a feature into something a merchant can picture.
Champion
30-second flip-card drills and a pitch builder, so the story sounds like you.
Industries
10 industries, each with the pain, the recipe, the questions to ask and the materials to send.
Grow and Tools
The leakage calculator, the deal path finder, the 28-slide presentation with a 14-slide Express cut, and the interactive demo.
Library
Every deck, playbook and template from the FlexiBill Drive, behind one search box.
