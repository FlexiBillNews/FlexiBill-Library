House rules

  • Describe competitors by type, never by name.
  • Never name insurance carriers in anything a merchant sees.
  • Never ask for credentials or production personal data in sales documents.

Classify every claim

Native and documented

Show it in the demo and say it plainly.

Configurable

Say what has to be set up, and by whom.

Integration-dependent

Name the system that must send or receive data.

Questions people ask

Do I need a Claude account?

No. IGNITE Arena, this guide, the Sales Hub and the libraries are ordinary web pages. Any browser works; Chrome works best.

A file asks me to request access.

Sign in with your DOKU Google account. If it still asks, email billingservice@doku.com with the file name and we’ll open it up.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser, on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it, and a different browser starts fresh.

Which countries does this page cover?

Malaysia, plus Singapore, Thailand, the Philippines and Vietnam. Only Malaysia is live; the others are proposed, so check the status before you promise anything.

Where do I start with a senangPay merchant?

Open the Migration room. It has the four motions, the activation checklist, what to say and the 12 FAQs, plus a playbook you can download.

Can I share IGNITE Arena with a merchant?

No, it’s internal. Share only materials meant for merchants, and check the claim label first.

Who keeps it up to date?

The FlexiBill product team. New versions replace the old one at the same link, so your bookmark keeps working.
