Three rules that survive every meeting

This deck is built to be fact-checked in the room. Every competitor line is drawn from that vendor's own public product and pricing documentation, and anything that is a judgement is written as one.

Name the substitute, not a vendor

Most deals are against a workaround — a spreadsheet, a static VA, a cron job — not against a logo. Ask what would stop working if the person who chases payments took a month off.

Concede before you claim

Say the competitor's real strength out loud, precisely, before anyone asks. A buyer can verify a concession instantly, which is what makes the next sentence believable.

Prove one cycle, then scope

One cohort, one billing cycle, measured. Collection rate, recovery rate, admin hours, days to cash. A measured cycle beats every card in this deck.
